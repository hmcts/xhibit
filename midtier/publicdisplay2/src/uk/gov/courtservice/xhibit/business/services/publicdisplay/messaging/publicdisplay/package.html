This package contains the two message driven beans used to couple the Public Display component to the existing Xhibit middle tier. They are present due to the fact that the rewrite of the Court Log Entry Event behaviour has not occurred and they should be replaced as this work is done.

Both beans are concerned with processing relevant Court Log Entry Events that are posted to Public Display relevant queues. In the case of the PublicDisplayNotification there are messages to do with other events, but these are handled by direct code in the xhibit middle tier.

PublicDisplayListener is concerned with events to do with standard court log events that cause re-renders of public display events. As far as I can tell at present, the mapping is to case status events, but that needs checking.

PublicNoticeListener is concerned with the events to do with public notices. There seem to be no complications to the mapping.
